What Is Red Cup Karaoke

It's Not Just
Karaoke.
It's a Party.

Red Cup Karaoke is Chicago's signature nightlife experience — where a full house loses their minds over the music, the mic, and the moment. This isn't your quiet bar trivia situation. This is a packed venue, a DJ-driven atmosphere, and an audience ready to ride for whoever steps on that stage.

Founded by FliNation with music by LMS and friends, every Red Cup event is a combination of community energy, iconic tracks, and unforgettable Chicago vibes. One red cup changes everything.
